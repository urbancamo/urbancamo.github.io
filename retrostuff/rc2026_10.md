# Retrochallenge 2026/10

## Goals

I have a PSI PP404 line printer, a stack of wide-carriage fanfold paper and two fresh printer ribbons.
My goal is to see how far I can push dot matrix printing, be it ascii art, interfacing it to old computers or
writing useful utilities. Probably a combination of all three.
